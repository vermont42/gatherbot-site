GatherBot

A private household assistant for one family.

GatherBot is a personal project, not a product or a public service. It helps one family keep track of school and activity emails, drafts email replies for a parent to review and send, and keeps the family's Google Calendar up to date. Family members talk to it through direct messages in a private Slack workspace.

Nobody outside the family can sign up for or use GatherBot.

See the privacy policy for what Google data GatherBot uses and how.
